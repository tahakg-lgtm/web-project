About Medusa Store

A premium catalog that stays on your device.

Medusa Store is a university web development project demonstrating a polished product browsing experience using only semantic HTML, modern CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

Fully offline

All product data and illustrations are packaged locally with no remote dependencies.

Personal browsing

Cart items, product ratings, and theme choice remain in local storage.

Built accessibly

Keyboard-friendly controls, clear focus states, responsive layouts, and considered contrast.
